## The budget: ₦300,000

My first public speaking event is getting real, so I sat down and worked out what it will cost. My budget is ₦300,000, and it covers:

- the hall
- my suit
- photos
- video

That is a big number for a first event. But I already knew one thing: I am not taking it from my savings.

## Funding it with my digital product

Savings are for security. They are not for funding every new idea. My plan is to raise the money by running ads for my digital product, [Cleaning Calculator Pro](https://selar.com/4to7446134), the tool I built to help cleaning business owners price their jobs properly.

The business pays for the business. If the product sells, the event is funded. This is how I want to build: one income stream feeding the next.

## The inspection fee story

Something else happened that I had to share. A new client wanted me to come and look at their space, and I charged a ₦4,000 inspection fee. They paid.

Many cleaning business owners feel shy about charging for inspections. They worry the client will walk away. But an inspection costs you transport, time and expertise. You are assessing the space, estimating the work and planning the job. That is professional work, and it deserves payment.

An inspection fee also filters out people who aren't serious. A client who pays to be inspected is a client who is ready to book.
