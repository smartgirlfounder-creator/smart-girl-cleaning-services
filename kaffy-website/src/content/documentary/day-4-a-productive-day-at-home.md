I didn't step out today, and that is fine.

There is a belief that you are only working when you are out: meeting clients, going to events, running around town. But some of my most productive days happen at home, with a plan and no distractions.

## What I got done

- **Shot my ads video.** I am getting ready to run ads for my digital product, so I filmed the video that will carry the message.
- **Shot my YouTube videos.** Batching my content saves me from scrambling later in the week.
- **Pitched my business to three notable people.** I reached out to three people whose work I respect and told them what I do. Pitching can feel uncomfortable, but it is how doors open.
- **Scheduled the Booked & Busy Challenge.** It runs from 6 to 9 November, for cleaning business owners who want more bookings.

## Intentional progress

Today wasn't loud, but it was intentional. Each task moves something forward: the ads move my product, the videos grow my audience, the pitches open doors, and the challenge brings my community together.

Progress doesn't always look exciting. Sometimes it is you at home, doing what you said you would do.
