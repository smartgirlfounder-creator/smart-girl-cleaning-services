Some days the work shows. Today was not one of those days.

If you had looked at me today, you would have seen a woman on her laptop for hours. No big announcement, no money landing in my account. But I was building, and I want this documentary to show those days too, because that is where most of the work actually happens.

## Setting up my website

A big part of today went into my website. If you have never built one, it takes far longer than people expect. You write the pages, fix what breaks, rewrite what doesn't sound like you, and do it again. By the time I stopped, the day had become the next morning.

I wanted a home on the internet that belongs to me: my story, my work, my products and this documentary, all in one place. Social media pages can be restricted or lost. A website is yours.

## Spending before seeing it come back

This is the part nobody puts on their highlight reel. Building something new costs money before it makes money. You pay first, and then you wait.

That waiting can make you doubt yourself. But an investment never looks like a return on the day you make it.

## Booking a space for my first speaking event

I also started planning to book a space for my first public speaking event. I have spoken to people online for a long time, but standing in a room in front of an audience I invited is a different kind of courage. I don't have every detail sorted yet. I am taking it one step at a time.

## Why cleaning still belongs on my page

As I grow into coaching, speaking and media, some people may ask why I still talk about cleaning. Cleaning is where this journey started. Smart Girl Cleaning Services taught me pricing, clients, staff and marketing, and everything I teach today came from it. Growing beyond cleaning doesn't mean leaving it behind.

It doesn't happen overnight. It's step by step.
