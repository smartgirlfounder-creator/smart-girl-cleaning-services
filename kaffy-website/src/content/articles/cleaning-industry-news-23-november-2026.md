## Nigeria is holding a national dialogue on faecal sludge

![A modern public toilet building on a city street (illustrative photo)](/images/articles/2026-11-23/public-toilet.webp)
*Illustrative photo: Emmanuel Codden on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/public-toilet-in-a-city-16665268/)*

**What happened:** The Minister of Environment, Balarabe Lawal, announced a National Dialogue on Faecal Sludge Management for 23 November, bringing together seven selected states across the six geopolitical zones and the Federal Capital Territory. Reports say the talks cover the collection, transport, treatment, disposal and reuse of faecal waste, and are meant to help states share experiences and agree practical recommendations. It follows World Toilet Day events in Abuja last week.

**My take:** When government starts talking seriously about how waste is collected and treated, it usually means new rules, new contracts and new standards are coming. If your cleaning business handles toilets, septic areas or post-construction sites, pay attention. Find out what your state environmental agency requires, get your team trained on safe handling, and keep your paperwork in order. The businesses that are ready when the rules arrive are the ones that win the contracts.

Read more: [News Agency of Nigeria](https://nannews.ng/?p=349259)

## Dutch cleaners end a two-week strike over their new contract

![Three cleaners in work uniforms carrying cleaning equipment (illustrative photo)](/images/articles/2026-11-23/cleaning-team.webp)
*Illustrative photo: Tima Miroshnichenko on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/cleaners-walking-while-holding-cleaning-equipment-6196677/)*

**What happened:** The Dutch union FNV called on cleaners across the Netherlands to stop work from 10 to 23 November in protest at a new collective agreement signed by the employers' group Schoonmakend Nederland and another union, CNV. FNV members had earlier rejected the deal, and one of their main complaints is reduced sick pay. The University of Amsterdam told students that essential cleaning would be maintained as much as possible during the strike.

**My take:** This story is about more than pay. It is about sick pay, the thing cleaners worry about when they are unwell and still have rent to pay. Even if you are a small business, think about what happens when one of your cleaners falls ill. A clear, fair policy, even a simple one, builds loyalty and stops good people leaving for the next offer. Write it down and explain it when you hire.

Read the full story: [Facto](https://www.facto.nl/45605/fnv-kondigt-twee-weken-stakingen-in-schoonmaak-aan)

## Spain will raise its minimum wage by 5.3% in 2027

![Euro banknotes spread out on a surface (illustrative photo)](/images/articles/2026-11-23/euro-banknotes.webp)
*Illustrative photo: Markus Spiske on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/photo-of-european-banknotes-4201343/)*

**What happened:** On 17 November, Spain's government approved a 5.3% rise in the national minimum wage for 2027, taking it to €1,286 a month, paid in 14 instalments. The new rate takes effect on 1 January 2027. The main unions had asked for 7% and did not back the final figure.

**My take:** In cleaning, labour is our biggest cost, so every wage rise lands straight on our margins. Whatever country you are in, don't wait for a new minimum wage to catch you by surprise. Build a yearly price review into your contracts, so you can adjust your rates when your costs go up. Clients accept increases much more easily when you agreed the review at the start.

Read the full story: [Catalan News](https://www.catalannews.com/society-science/item/spain-raises-minimum-wage-53-to-1286-a-month-for-2027)

## Argentina's domestic workers get another pay rise

![A housekeeper in uniform ironing a white shirt (illustrative photo)](/images/articles/2026-11-23/housekeeper-ironing.webp)
*Illustrative photo: Liliana Drew on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-person-ironing-clothes-9462667/)*

**What happened:** Minimum wages for people who work in private homes in Argentina, including cleaners and housekeepers, rose by 1.6% in November under a scheduled increase. Reports say the minimum monthly wage for general household work, for workers who live out, is now just over 512,000 pesos, with a further step planned for December. Workers in certain regions receive an extra 31% zone allowance.

**My take:** I like that Argentina publishes clear pay scales for domestic work, so both workers and households know what is fair. Many of us in Nigeria don't have that, which is exactly why you should set your own. Decide your pay rates by role, write them down, and review them every year. When your team knows where they stand, there are fewer arguments and less staff turnover.

Read the full story: [El Economista](https://eleconomista.com.ar/economia/empleadas-domesticas-aumentan-sueldos-noviembre-categoria-supera-620000-quienes-cobran-31-adicional-n99416)

## South Africa's police service is hiring 33 cleaners

![A person mopping a wooden floor with a flat mop (illustrative photo)](/images/articles/2026-11-23/mopping-floor.webp)
*Illustrative photo: Polina Tankilevitch on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-person-cleaning-the-floor-with-a-mop-4440568/)*

**What happened:** The South African Police Service has advertised 33 cleaner posts at its Forensic Science Laboratories in Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal, the Eastern Cape and the Western Cape. The posts were listed on 20 November, close on 4 December and require Grade 9 or ABET Level 4. Reports put the salary at R144,024 a year.

**My take:** Forensic laboratories are spaces where the wrong cleaning can ruin evidence, so these jobs need trained, careful people. That is a reminder that specialist cleaning (labs, clinics, kitchens, server rooms) is valued more highly than general cleaning. If you want better-paying contracts, pick one specialist area, train your team for it and market it clearly.
