## Nigeria now has 185 open defecation-free local governments

![Hands being washed with soap and water poured from a jug (illustrative photo)](/images/articles/2026-11-20/hand-washing-soap-water.webp)
*Illustrative photo: Israël Umba on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-person-washing-hands-with-soap-and-water-3995736/)*

**What happened:** At a press briefing in Abuja on 16 November, ahead of World Toilet Day, the Minister of Water Resources and Sanitation, Joseph Utsev, said 185 local government areas in Nigeria have now been certified open defecation-free, with Jigawa and Katsina reaching that status statewide. He said the government is aiming to end open defecation across the country by 2030, and that a presidential executive order on the goal was due to be unveiled by Vice-President Kashim Shettima on World Toilet Day, 19 November.

**My take:** When government starts talking seriously about sanitation, money and contracts follow. Toilets have to be built, but they also have to be cleaned and maintained, in schools, markets, motor parks and public buildings. If you run a cleaning business in Nigeria, start building your experience with washroom cleaning and hygiene services now, and keep records and photos of the work you do. When those contracts come, the companies with proof of experience will be first in line.

## A humanoid robot that tidies up before the floor robots clean

![A robot vacuum cleaner on a wooden floor (illustrative photo)](/images/articles/2026-11-20/robot-vacuum-floor.webp)
*Illustrative photo: Kindel Media on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/black-vacuum-cleaner-on-hardwood-floor-8566448/)*

**What happened:** Robot makers used the ISSA Show North America in Las Vegas (17–19 November) to show off new machines. In a press release on 19 November, KEENON Robotics described a set-up where a humanoid robot clears clutter and tidies tabletops first, and then its floor robots sweep and scrub. KEENON also showed delivery and transport robots designed to work alongside its cleaning robots. These details come from the company's own announcement.

**My take:** Notice the pattern: robots are being built for the big, flat, repetitive jobs, such as large floors in offices, hotels and airports. They are not built for the detail work: clutter, corners, kitchens and bathrooms. That detail work is where small cleaning businesses should become excellent. And if you clean large commercial spaces, start learning how these machines work. One day soon a client will ask whether you can work alongside one.

Read the full announcement: [KEENON on PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/keenon-robotics-showcases-multi-robot-collaboration-and-explores-humanoid-assisted-cleaning-at-issa-2026-302945381.html)

## Hotel housekeepers competed in the Housekeeping Olympics

![A hotel housekeeper making a bed in a modern room (illustrative photo)](/images/articles/2026-11-20/hotel-housekeeper-making-bed.webp)
*Illustrative photo: Ken Mwaura on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/hotel-staff-making-bed-in-modern-room-29006838/)*

**What happened:** Housekeeping teams from Las Vegas hotels competed in the 36th ISSA Housekeeping Olympics on 16 November, held alongside the ISSA Show. LIV Hospitality won the overall title, with Excalibur second and Mandalay Bay third. Teams competed in eight events, including a new triathlon.

**My take:** I love this, because cleaners rarely get celebrated. You don't need a stadium to do the same for your team. Run a small in-house challenge: fastest perfect bed, best-finished bathroom, neatest kit. Give a small prize and post the winners (with their permission). Recognition costs very little, and it helps you keep good cleaners.

## South Africa lost about 31,000 domestic worker jobs in three months

![Gloved hands wiping a kitchen counter (illustrative photo)](/images/articles/2026-11-20/gloved-hands-kitchen-counter.webp)
*Illustrative photo: RDNE Stock project on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-person-cleaning-a-kitchen-counter-5591908/)*

**What happened:** Figures from Statistics South Africa's labour survey for the third quarter of 2026, reported last week, show around 31,000 fewer domestic worker jobs than in the previous quarter, leaving about 800,000 people employed in the sector. Reports describe it as one of the lowest levels since the COVID-19 pandemic. BusinessTech linked the fall to anti-foreigner protests and migrant workers leaving the country, while earlier coverage has pointed to tight household budgets.

**My take:** When households cut back on full-time domestic workers, many of them still need cleaning, just less of it. That is an opening for cleaning businesses that offer flexible packages: a weekly or fortnightly clean, or a once-a-month deep clean, instead of a full-time worker. Make your pricing simple and your packages small enough that a stretched household can still say yes.

Read the full story: [BusinessTech](https://businesstech.co.za/news/?p=883614)

## A janitorial company is offering free walkthrough estimates

![A blank to-do list on a clipboard (illustrative photo)](/images/articles/2026-11-20/clipboard-to-do-list.webp)
*Illustrative photo: MART PRODUCTION on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-to-do-list-on-a-clipboard-7718755/)*

**What happened:** Clear Choice Janitorial, a family-owned commercial cleaning company near Sacramento, California, put out a press release this week celebrating more than 120 Google reviews. In it, the company promotes free on-site walkthrough estimates for businesses in Sacramento and nearby cities. This is the company's own announcement.

**My take:** This is a lesson in pricing, and I say it often: never quote a commercial job blind. Two offices of the same size can need completely different amounts of work. Visit, walk the space, take notes and photos, and ask how many people use it every day. Then price it. A walkthrough also shows the client you are serious, and that alone wins jobs.

Read the full story: [The Manila Times (press release)](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/17/tmt-newswire/plentisoft/clear-choice-janitorial-surpasses-120-google-reviews-reinforcing-its-standing-among-the-top-commercial-cleaning-services-in-sacramento/2471553)

## Quick tip of the week

The end of the year is when households and offices want everything spotless for visitors and celebrations. Don't wait for clients to ask. This week, send your regular clients a short message offering a year-end deep clean, and give them dates to choose from, so your diary fills up before the rush.
