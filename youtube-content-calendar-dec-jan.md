## 2. Your weekly rhythm (no daily lives)

You can film 5 videos a day, but **upload 1 long video a day, not 5.** Five uploads on the same day compete with each other for the same viewers, and that's why so many November uploads got 10–20 views.

| Day | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Mon & Thu (filming days)** | Batch-film 3–4 long videos each day (20–40 min each). That's your whole week + extras in the bank |
| **Every day (Mon–Sat)** | Upload **1 long video** at the same time (e.g. 6 pm WAT) + **2–3 Shorts cut from it**, each linked to the full video with "Related video" |
| **Sunday** | **1 live Q&A only** (60–90 min): "Ask Me Anything About Starting a Business". Title + thumbnail planned, replay kept public |
| Tue/Wed/Fri spare time | Thumbnails, titles, replying to comments, sharing to WhatsApp community/Instagram/email list |

**Length rules:** at least 20 minutes. One "mega" video (45–60+ min) every week.
**Never** split one topic into 4 short videos. Make it one long video with chapters.

## 3. Four video structures (pick one per title)

**A. STORY / CHALLENGE** ("I started with ₦0…", "How my life changed…")
0:00 the result first ("30 days later, this happened") → 0:30 where I started (the low point) → day-by-day or step-by-step what I did → the mistakes → the numbers → "here's how you can copy it" (steps) → end screen.

**B. MONEY LIST** ("3 services that pay…", "7 boring businesses…")
0:00 "there are X; #1 surprised me, I'll show it last" → each item: what it is, startup cost, what it pays, who pays, how to get the first job → rank them → best one revealed last → end screen.

**C. COMPLETE GUIDE** ("…Complete Guide 2027", "Start in 7 days")
0:00 what you'll be able to do by the end → numbered steps (put the step count on screen) → real examples/screenshots from your business → common mistakes → checklist recap → end screen. Add chapters.

**D. CHECKLIST / MISTAKES** ("Don't take your first client until…", "Everyone skips this")
0:00 the costly mistake story → the checklist items one by one with "why it matters" → the one most people skip (saved for last) → end screen.

**Every video:** no "welcome back to my channel" intro, give the hook within 10 seconds, and **say the number of steps/items upfront** so people stay.
