A stone countertop is only as good as its installation. This guide walks you through each stage, so you know what to expect and how to prepare.

## Step 1: Plan your kitchen

Decide your layout, cabinet positions, sink and hob. Choose the stone (marble, granite or quartz) and the look you want. If you are unsure, read our [quartz vs marble vs granite comparison](/blog/quartz-vs-marble-vs-granite-kitchen-countertops/).

## Step 2: Install the base cabinets

Base cabinets must be fixed, strong and level before the countertop is measured. Any movement or unevenness will show in the stone.

## Step 3: Site measurement

We visit to measure every run, corner and island precisely and confirm the sink, hob and tap positions. Accurate measurement is the foundation of a good fit.

## Step 4: Fabrication

The stone is cut to size, edges are shaped and polished, and openings are cut for the sink, hob and taps. Undermount sinks need especially careful, polished cut-outs.

## Step 5: Installation

The pieces are positioned on the cabinets, levelled and secured. Joints are bonded and finished, and edges are polished on site where needed. Upstands or backsplashes are fitted last.

## Step 6: Finishing and handover

We clean the surface, check levels and joints, and explain how to care for your new countertop.

## How to prepare for installation day

- Make sure cabinets are installed and empty
- Clear access to the kitchen for carrying heavy stone
- Have the sink and hob on site or their exact sizes ready
- Arrange for power to be available
