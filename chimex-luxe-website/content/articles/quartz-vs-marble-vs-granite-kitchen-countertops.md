Quartz, marble and granite are the three most popular materials for premium kitchen countertops. Here is how they compare, in plain language.

## What each material is

- **Marble** is a natural stone known for elegant veining. It is softer than granite and sensitive to acids.
- **Granite** is a hard natural stone with a speckled or patterned look. Every slab is unique.
- **Quartz** worktops are engineered from crushed natural quartz bound with resin. The colour and pattern are consistent.

## Side-by-side comparison

| | Marble | Granite | Quartz |
|---|---|---|---|
| Look | Soft, flowing veins | Speckled, patterned, many colours | Uniform, modern |
| Every slab unique | Yes | Yes | Mostly consistent |
| Scratch resistance | Lower | High | High |
| Heat | Use trivets for very hot pots | Handles heat well | Protect from very hot pans |
| Acids (lemon, vinegar) | Can etch | Resistant | Resistant |
| Sealing | Needed periodically | Needed periodically | Not needed |
| Outdoor use | Possible with care | Good | Not usually recommended in direct sun |

## Which should you choose?

- **For heavy everyday cooking:** granite is the practical choice.
- **For a uniform, modern, easy-clean look:** quartz.
- **For a classic, luxurious statement:** marble, if you are happy to care for it.

Many kitchens mix materials, for example granite on the cooking run and a marble or quartz island.
