Marble rewards a little care with years of beauty. These simple habits will keep your kitchen marble tops looking their best.

## Daily cleaning

- Wipe the surface with a soft cloth and warm water.
- Use a mild, pH-neutral cleaner made for stone, or a drop of gentle dish soap.
- Dry with a soft cloth to avoid water marks.

## What to avoid

- **Acids:** lemon juice, vinegar, tomato, soft drinks and many bathroom or multi-surface cleaners can etch polished marble.
- **Abrasives:** scouring pads and gritty powders can scratch the surface.
- **Bleach and harsh chemicals:** they can dull or damage the stone.
- **Cutting directly on the stone:** always use a chopping board.

## Spills and stains

Wipe spills immediately, especially oil, palm oil, stew, wine and coffee. Blot rather than rub. Coasters under glasses and trays under oil bottles help prevent rings.

## Heat

Use trivets or mats under very hot pots and pans straight from the fire.

## Sealing

Sealing helps marble resist stains. A simple test: drop a little water on the surface. If it soaks in and darkens the stone quickly, it is time to reseal. Use a sealer made for natural stone and follow the product instructions.

## Etch marks

Dull spots from acids are called etches. Light etching on polished marble can often be improved by professional polishing. Ask us if you need help.
