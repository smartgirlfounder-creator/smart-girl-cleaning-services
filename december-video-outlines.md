## Every video follows the same shape
1. **Hook (first 15 seconds):** say the result + the promise. No "welcome back to my channel".
2. **Proof (30–60 sec):** one quick real moment from your business.
3. **Say the number upfront:** "There are 5 steps…", "I'll give you 3…"
4. **Main body:** one section per step (these become your chapters).
5. **Open loop:** "The last one is the one that changed everything for me, so stay to the end."
6. **End (last 20 sec):** "Now watch this video next" → end screen. No long goodbye.
