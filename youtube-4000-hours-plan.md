- **Your real face takes up 40–50% of the frame**, close-up, with a clear emotion (confident smile, skeptical look, shock).
- **3–5 words max**, bold white sans-serif, + **one bright yellow bar** with the money number/key word.
- Simple blurred background (your home, office or a clean room). Your signature **mustard/yellow** color ties your brand together (your best thumbnail uses it).
