A client sends you a message: "How much for a deep clean of my 3-bedroom flat?"

You think for a second and reply: ₦45,000. It feels fair. They agree. You're happy.

But let's do the maths on that job.

Step 1: Add up what the job really costs you (example numbers)

  • Staff: 3 cleaners × ₦8,000 = ₦24,000
  • Feeding: 3 × ₦2,000 = ₦6,000
  • Transport: ₦5,000
  • Materials and chemicals: ₦7,000
  • Equipment wear: ₦2,000

Job cost: ₦44,000

Step 2: Add overhead (about 10%)
Your data, phone, marketing and everything else that keeps your business running: ₦4,400

Real cost: ₦48,400

Step 3: Add your profit (25–35%)
At 30%, that's about ₦14,500.

Step 4: Round to a clean figure
Your price: ₦63,000

So that ₦45,000 "fair" price? You didn't just make no profit. You lost ₦3,400 and worked a full day for it.

This is why so many cleaning businesses are always busy but never have money. It's not a client problem. It's a pricing problem.
